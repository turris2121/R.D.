USD/MXN: odds by horizon

The centre is the forward rate, the level that makes holding CETES or T-bills break even. It's what the market prices in, not a forecast. The width comes from the peso's volatility over the last year. Historically the peso has often beaten its forward because of the rate gap (the "carry"), so the odds of a weaker peso may be overstated.

How these numbers are made

  • Calendars. FOMC dates come from federalreserve.gov; Banxico dates from its published calendar. Past decisions compare the rate the day before each meeting with the rate 5 days after.
  • Odds (estimate). If a bank moves 25 bp at a meeting k days away, the average rate over a bill's life of h days moves by 25 bp × (h−k)/h. So the bill yield, minus its usual spread to the policy rate, gives the probability-weighted move. The Fed uses T-bills against the effective fed funds rate; Banxico uses CETES against its target. The usual spread ("basis") is the median from the last 2 years, counting only days when the rate didn't change during the bill's life. This is cruder than futures-based tools: cross-check with CME FedWatch.
  • USD/MXN odds. Forward = FIX × (1 + CETES × d/360) ÷ (1 + T-bill × d/360). Outcomes are modelled as lognormal around the forward, with the last 252 days' realized volatility of the FIX. Options-implied volatility would be better, but there's no free source for it.
  • Tasa objetivo = (GBM Smart Cash rate + Banxico target) ÷ 2. GBM has no public API, so its rate is entered by hand when you start the workflow.
  • Sources. US: New York Fed and US Treasury, with FRED as fallback. Mexico: Banxico SIE API. Bill yields are the latest published, usually one business day old; CETES come from weekly auctions.
